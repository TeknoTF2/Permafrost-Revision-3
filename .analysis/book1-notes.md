# PERMAFROST Book 1 — Reading Notes (Rev 3)

~95,751 words, 30 chapters. POV: rotating limited 3rd person among the five party members, scene breaks "* * *". Internal thoughts in 'single quotes'.

## Premise
A magical dome has covered the country of Kordova, blotting the sun, causing deepening supernatural cold, suppressing magic and divine connection. Party of five woke ~3 weeks before ch1 in/near village Velldale (pop 206, Thane Aldrich, 18 days of food).

## Party
- **Muse** — blind human prophet of the Solar Covenant (goddess Naika, sun goddess). Sealed underground ~1000 years with Cain. Blindfold over ruined eyes ("If Naika takes your sight, she makes your hands more certain"). CENTRAL SECRET: Naika is silent/gone; Muse lies to Cain ("fragments... she's reaching") to protect his faith. Performs "the prophet's voice." Heals by touch, sutures blind. Navigates by sound/touch, hand on Cain's belt.
- **Cain** — satyr paladin of Naika, 7 ft, plate armor, shield+spear, buys halberd in Crimson Port. One eye (eyepatch). Ancient soldier; sealed w/ Muse ~1000 yrs. Vow: never take another HUMAN life (monsters okay). Trauma: Solar Covenant genocide ordered by Lord **Barbatos**; temples burned while Cain was lured away by manufactured border skirmish. Panic attack/vomiting in alley (ch9) - hides weakness, "a wall that cracks doesn't protect anything." Tactical mind, reads group as a unit.
- **Carina** — 19, human(?) martial artist from Murim (another continent), Mount Hua sect; mother is sect head, "strongest person alive." Silver hair, purple eyes. Cultivator (ki, meridians, herbs: ginseng, red sage, astragalus). Restless, brash, coin-flipping, comic motor-mouth. Might-makes-right worldview: weak leaders deserve removal. CHILLING reveal ch10-11: perfect stillness, syncs breathing to sleeping target, "done this before"; kills the maid hostage without hesitation (punches through her head to hit Andromalius) — party horrified. Black-and-white morality, doesn't grasp why others hesitate.
- **Gormund** — dwarf craftsman/refugee from Zyphoria (war-torn; Thornwall refugee line; village firebombed weekly; learned field medicine on frontlines; once built/carried a RIFLE for 6 years). Crossbow + hammer. Whiskey flask (functional alcoholic, manages trauma). Fixes everyone's gear unasked (Muse's boots/gloves). Pessimist-pragmatist with deep compassion. Minor healing magic (untrained, mana sickness). Kills the dragon by throwing hammer into its throat mid-breath ("Open wide").
- **Uthesia** — eladrin, one eye, widow (wife's grave - first thing she'll do post-dome). Quiet, precise, kicks-based fighter w/ force-redirection technique (catching dragon paws; nosebleeds at limit). Summoner: binds spirits to scout. Hates Lords of the 72 — lost loved ones because "a Lord decided they were less important than his convenience." Deadpan wit ("You must take after your father"). Hidden agenda hinted (listening-quiet; mental inventory of supply chains).

## World
- Kordova: the domed country. Settlements: Velldale (village) → Crimson Port (harbor city, Harbormaster, decent governance, rationing, refugee lines, ~60 days provisions) → Valdara (financial hub, pop 120k→50k, Lord Andromalius) → west: Wolkenfeld → Bloomridge (silent) → Solarspire (capital, silent since dome).
- The 72 Lords: hereditary bloodline aristocracy descended from 72 heroes who "pulled civilization from the dark." Ranked 1-72. Bloodline grants superhuman strength/longevity ("above kings"). Now mostly decadent. Barbatos = rank "somewhere in the tens", destroyed Solar Covenant ~1000 yrs ago, may still be alive (immortal).
- Solar Covenant: erased faith. Naika = sun goddess ("flame of our hearts... light that does not abandon us"). Andromalius has never heard of it.
- Murim: Carina's continent, martial sects (Mount Hua), strength-based legitimacy.
- Dome: suppresses magic (healing costs more), kills sun/warmth, frost deepens toward interior/epicenter. No birds. Animals starving/desperate.
- Mysterious dragging sound following party at night from the north (ch2,3,6) — unresolved thread.

## Prose/craft notes so far
- Strengths: sensory writing in Muse POV (blind perception via sound/touch/temperature); distinct voice per POV; banter lands; thematic coherence (math of survival, lines/queues, who deserves what); foreshadowing discipline (bear hide, dragging sound, Carina's stillness); moral complexity executed through character not lecture.
- Tics: "the kind that/of" construction frequent; "something" vagueness; em-dash interruptions; 'X didn't... It was worse' construction; characters cataloguing/filing things away; "like a" similes heavy; thought-italics with 'quotes'; repetition of "the prophet's voice"; lots of "Hm."
- Maid-kill is THE shock moment - genre-aware readers will split. Tone: D&D-party-meets-grimdark-survival; Murim/cultivation crossover element is unusual genre fusion (LitRPG-adjacent wuxia x western fantasy).
