Dialogue Density

Dialogue % per chapter

The near-zero chapters are deliberate (B1 Ch. 13–16: the dungeon fights; B1 Ch. 21: Carina dying alone — 0% dialogue, 20.4% interior monologue; B2 Ch. 18: the dream). The data confirms a stylistic signature: this series goes silent at its emotional extremes. Useful self-knowledge — when a chapter is dragging, the fix is usually to let people talk.

Sentence Rhythm

Average sentence length per chapter (words)

A visible compression trend across Book One (15.7 → ~11) as the story darkens — sentences shorten under stress, which is craft working as intended. Book Two holds a tight 10–14 band. Watch for monotony in long action stretches: several fight chapters sit at 10–11 words/sentence for thousands of words; an occasional long, rolling sentence buys the short ones their punch back.

Adverbs

Top -ly adverbs

~1,666 true -ly adverbs ≈ 7.2 per 1,000 words — within professional range (typical published fantasy: 6–12/1k; problem manuscripts: 15+). The leaders are hedge-adverbs of precision ("barely" 88, "actually" 82, "exactly" 75, "probably" 74, "slightly" 42) rather than dialogue-tag offenders — consistent with the narrating-analyst voice of the cast. Trim "slightly/barely" pairs in fight scenes where the choreography already implies the margin.

Filter Words

Verbs that put the narrator between the reader and the experience ("she felt the wind" vs. "the wind cut her"). Combined: felt/feel 371 · looked 641 · watched 185 · heard 163 · saw 56 · knew 113 · realized 29 · noticed 31. Two notes: (1) Muse's POV legitimately requires heard/felt at elevated rates — exclude her chapters from the sweep; (2) "saw" being rare while "looked/watched" run hot suggests the habit is staging characters' gazes, not filtering perception — fix with action beats, not deletion.

Most-Used Meaningful Words

Top content words (stopwords removed)

The vocabulary fingerprint is the book's thesis in noun form: hand/hands (863 combined), body (417), people (414), voice (403), cold (340), wall (330), sound (301), stone (295). A tactile, embodied, acoustic lexicon — almost no abstract nouns in the top 40. That's the blind-POV discipline radiating through the whole manuscript. ("Through" at 938 is the hidden tell: this is a book about getting through things — walls, ice, bodies, grief.)

Interior Monologue

Quoted-italic thought runs ~2.1% of Book One and ~1.3% of Book Two by word count — modest on average, but concentrated: B1 Ch. 21 (Carina dying) is 20.4% interiority and B2 Ch. 18 (the dream) 20.2%. The two highest-interiority chapters are also the two highest-rated chapters in this report. The style knows what it's doing; the only caution is mid-scene "explainer thoughts" that restate a feeling the action just dramatized (audited per-chapter in the chapter pages).

Data Verdict

The macro numbers (chapter lengths, adverb rate, beat-word discipline, hook percentage) look like a professionally edited book already. The line-level repetition cluster — looked / something / enough / silence-quiet / voice / felt — is the one measurable gap between this draft and published prose, and it is a search-and-revise problem, not a talent problem. One focused pass, chapter by chapter with these six lists open, moves the prose score from 7 to 8.5.
